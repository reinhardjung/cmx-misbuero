Office workflows for WordPress: contacts, articles, invoices, documents, projects and budget.

== Description ==

Mis Buero provides a small office workspace inside WordPress. The free plugin focuses on core workflows that are useful without paid add-ons:

* Contacts
* Articles
* Invoices and documents
* Projects
* Budget
* Basic settings

Business modules such as bookings, banking/CAMT, email, calendar, automation and advanced reports are distributed separately and are not bundled in this free plugin.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin files to `/wp-content/plugins/mis-buero`, or install the ZIP through the WordPress plugins screen.
2. Activate the plugin through the Plugins screen.
3. Open the Mis Buero admin menu and create your first contact, article or invoice.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does the free plugin require an external service? =

No. The free plugin is intended to run as a standalone WordPress plugin.

= Are business modules included but locked? =

No. Business modules are separate add-ons and are not bundled as locked trial features.
